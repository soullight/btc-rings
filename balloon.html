Stretch is one number: how inflated bitcoin is relative to its own history. 0 is maximum contraction, 100 is maximum expansion. It describes a state, not a forecast — it says where, never when. Not advice.
Price ticks live. The stretch reading carries its own timestamp above and turns amber when it ages.
