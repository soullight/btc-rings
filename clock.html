Collected server-side on the Nevada box every 20 minutes, 24/7 — Binance (price, volume), Bitfinex (margin longs/shorts, funding), alternative.me (Fear & Greed), and the chain read straight off Lawrence's own full node. Stored in clock.db, published here as clock-data.json. If the stamp above goes red, the box stopped — not the market. Cycle dates measured on real daily highs/lows: lows 18 Nov 2011 · 14 Jan 2015 · 15 Dec 2018 · 21 Nov 2022 · tops 30 Nov 2013 · 17 Dec 2017 · 10 Nov 2021 · 6 Oct 2025 · halvings 28 Nov 2012 · 9 Jul 2016 · 11 May 2020 · 19 Apr 2024. Refreshes every 60s. Nothing here is a projection — it is where today sits between dated events.
