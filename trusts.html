True NAV premium / discount

This is the number the report above could never give you: market price against the assets each trust actually holds. Holdings and share counts come from each trust's own 10-Q; the discount is price ÷ (holdings-per-share × spot) − 1. Negative means you buy a dollar of coin for less than a dollar.

◀ discount · cheappremium · overpaying ▶
Two of these are traps. GLNK and GXLM trade at +74% and +69% over the Chainlink and Stellar they hold — pay $1.74 for a dollar of LINK. Both sit at 0–2% on the range chart above, indistinguishable from the genuinely beaten-down names. Range position and value are different questions and the first one cannot answer the second.

BCHG and LTCN each did a 7.5:1 share split. Using the balance-sheet share count instead of the current one reports them at −88%, which is not real — no Grayscale trust has ever traded below ETHE's record −59%. Corrected here against post-split shares.
What a discount is worth. $100,000 into ETCG buys about $149,000 of Ethereum Classic. Against that, every trust charges 2.5%/yr and the coin-per-share shrinks accordingly — a 33% discount is roughly 13 years of fee, a 6% discount about two. The discount closes for real only if the trust converts to an ETF, which is what GBTC did in January 2024 and what ZCSH has filed to do on NYSE Arca.
