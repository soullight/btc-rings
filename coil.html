The Coil · realised volatility

—

Volatility is just how much price moves day to day. When it collapses, the market has gone quiet — no panic, no euphoria, nobody being forced to do anything. Quiet never lasts. This is the one measurement that says whether the violence is over.

—
7-day realised volatility, annualised, computed from the 5-minute price collector (market_snapshots). State only — no forecast, no direction, no date.
